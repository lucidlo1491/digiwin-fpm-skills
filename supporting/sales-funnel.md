# Digiwin Sales Funnel — Agent Reference

## Stages (E → D → C2 → C1 → B → A)

| Stage | Customer State | Sales Action | Stall SLA |
|-------|---------------|-------------|-----------|
| **E** | Target, no pain awareness | Send materials, invite to events, maintain contact | 14 days |
| **D** | Feels pain, doesn't know solution | Visit, scenario questions, expand pain, find power person | 30 days |
| **C2** | Knows solution, no internal consensus | Reach power person, pain chain, multi-dept alignment, seminars | 30 days |
| **C1** | Project established, evaluating vendors | Demo, confirm requirements/timeline/budget, contact decision-maker | 45 days |
| **B** | Proposal submitted, competing | Executive briefing, case study visits, negotiate price | 30 days |
| **A** | Decision made, signing this month | Remove last concerns, finalize contract | 14 days |

## Non-Negotiable Reasons (非做不可的理由)

The 4 real motivations that drive ERP purchases:
1. New factory with no existing system
2. Labor costs too high (need automation)
3. Internal controls failing (theft, material loss)
4. Generational succession (new management wants modernization)

If none of these exist, the deal may not be real.

## Key Concept: Pain vs Frustration

> Pain = pressure from others (boss demands reports that can't be produced). Drives action.
> Frustration = mild annoyance (things are slow). Does NOT drive action.
> The salesperson's job: find pain, expand it to organizational consequences.
