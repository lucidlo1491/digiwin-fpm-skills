### Resolved decisions (the grill record)
1. **Tiered trust** — the human gate moves to the ARTIFACT. Decks/briefs may
   draft from unreviewed (graded+flagged) transcripts with unverified marks;
   full review is required only for the permanent record (verified facts,
   Obsidian, anything reported upward). Transcript perfectionism is no
   longer a precondition for motion.
2. **Decks:** proposal deck (提案報告, proven process) first → evolves into
   ONE LIVING DECK PER OPPORTUNITY, updated pre-visit (OSINT) and
   post-meeting (facts). **Every generation = an immutable version** with
   date + source-meeting provenance.
3. **Data model correction:** one company ≠ one deal. Opportunities =
   company × product-line (TN/ERP, TN/eMES…), each with own stage, facts,
   cadence, deck. Meetings attach to company; facts route to opportunity.
4. **Contact cadence rule:** C2/C1 opportunities must be touched weekly —
   Today + digest surface "⚠ C1 · X — N days silent." E/D breathe free.
5. **Distributors are first-class** (type: direct | distributor), same
   machinery, NEVER merged in one view (two tabs). Distributor-specific
   qualification taxonomy deferred until volume demands it.

## Information architecture (Release 1)
**Four destinations:** `Today` · `Meetings` · `Deals` · `Search` (one box).
- **Today** (landing): processing-now strip · review queue · proposed facts
  awaiting verify. Empty = "Nothing needs you." Nothing else.
- **Meetings:** the full table, status filters.
- **Deals:** deal index (stage · verified/missing elements · last meeting).
  Deal page = progressive disclosure: verified facts first, gaps as ONE line
  ("Missing: 預算 · 時程 · 競爭" — the prep list), proposed queue prominent,
  OSINT/artifacts collapsed until clicked.
- **Settings & Tools:** glossary, Digiwin team, credits, theme, rename/merge,
  runbook. Demoted from the sidebar junk drawer.
- Sidebar = 4 destinations + pinned deals + upload zone. That's all.
- Editor toolbar: Play · Next ? · Save · Mark Reviewed + ⋯ (speed, heatmap,
  find, translation, Top-20).
